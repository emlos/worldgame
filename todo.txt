game systems:
    player stats:
    - postpone the full stats rework until there is enough content to exercise and balance it
    - do not restore the mind stat; it overlaps with stress, fear, trauma, energy, and resolve
    - keep body condition and three baseline meters permanently visible:
        - condition: a qualitative physical state derived from weighted body-part integrity; do not present it as pooled health
        - energy: the basic currency for interacting with the world; spent by time and effort and restored by rest
        - stress: accumulated difficulty coping; rises and recovers over hours or days rather than behaving like a momentary emotion
        - hygiene: bodily cleanliness; changed mainly by tagged activities rather than uniform passive decay
    - show other states only while relevant:
        - pain: derived from persistent injury pain plus short-lived acute pain on each body part
        - comfort: derived from temperature, weather, wetness, clothing, hygiene, pain, shelter, furniture, and sleeping conditions
        - fear: a fast-changing response to an identifiable threat; darkness alone should not automatically cause it
        - anger: a fast-changing emotional state that can be useful as well as harmful
        - arousal: a temporary physiological state used by intimate/sexual content
        - air: a scene-local emergency resource for underwater, choking, and suffocation situations
    - represent lasting trauma as specific experiences, memories, triggers, and traits instead of a generic 0-100 meter
        - example: nearly drowned -> deep-water/breath-restraint triggers, fear gain, avoidance, intrusive memories
        - severe stress can increase vulnerability, but ordinary accumulated stress should not automatically become trauma
        - allow traumatic experiences to be processed through time, support, therapy, or authored events
    - stress incentives and possible bands:
        - 0-29 settled: normal function and good emotional recovery
        - 30-59 strained: fear and anger accumulate slightly faster
        - 60-79 overwhelmed: concentration suffers and relaxation/sleep become less effective
        - 80-99 near breaking: contextual crisis responses are threatened
        - 100 breaking point: trigger an appropriate interrupt such as panic, shutdown, flight, or an outburst
        - do not reward maintaining exactly zero; the goal is to keep stress manageable
        - provide dependable recovery through sleep, quiet activities, entertainment, exercise, supportive conversations, and resolving responsibilities
    - hygiene incentives and possible bands:
        - 70-100 clean: no penalty and occasional contextual benefits
        - 40-69 presentable: ordinary state
        - 15-39 dirty: noticeable reactions during close social situations
        - 0-14 filthy: strong reactions and problems with customer-facing work
        - low hygiene should influence NPC reactions, intimacy, jobs/school, comfort, and contextual prose
        - eventually consider separate internal body hygiene and clothing cleanliness while presenting one general status
    - stat/state interplay:
        - low energy can raise stress when the player continues exerting themself
        - stress amplifies fear and anger gain and slows their recovery
        - relevant trauma triggers amplify fear/stress; unrelated trauma should not penalize every threatening scene
        - pain increases physical-action energy costs, disrupts sleep/concentration, and raises stress when prolonged
        - adrenaline from fear can temporarily mask pain or effort, followed by an energy cost; fear should not directly generate energy
        - anger can enable intimidation, confrontation, or decisive action and temporarily suppress fear/pain, while harming precision, empathy, and de-escalation
        - low comfort hinders stress recovery; high comfort supports relaxation and sleep
        - low hygiene affects stress through discomfort or social context rather than constantly adding stress in every situation
        - very low air first raises fear, then causes impairment, unconsciousness, and physical damage
        - arousal affects physiological responses and intimate-scene progression but is never equivalent to consent, attraction, enjoyment, or willingness
    - preserve player agency: fear, anger, and stress may change available approaches or threaten consequences, but should only force involuntary behavior at clearly telegraphed extremes
    - if a single value is needed for a pressured action, derive composure from resolve, support, comfort, stress, fear, and pain instead of storing another stat
